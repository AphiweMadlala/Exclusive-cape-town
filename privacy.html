Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information when you visit this site or enquire about a residence.

Last updated — August 2026

Who we are

Exclusive Cape Town ("we", "us", "our") curates and markets a private collection of luxury villas, apartments and estates across Cape Town and the Western Cape, South Africa. This policy explains what personal information this website collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. It's written to be read alongside the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

Information we collect

This website does not use accounts, logins, or an online booking or payment system — enquiries and bookings are handled directly and personally. The only personal information we receive is what you choose to share with us, typically when you:

  • Send an enquiry via WhatsApp, phone, or email using the contact details on this site;
  • Message us directly on Instagram.

This may include your name, contact details, travel dates, group size, and any other information you include in your message. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and this site is not directed at children.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers, and does not build a profile of your browsing activity. No non-essential tracking technology runs on this site, so no cookie consent banner is shown.

Third-party services used by this site

A small number of external services are used purely to make the site function, and each may process standard technical information (such as your IP address) as part of normal web delivery:

  • Google Fonts — used to load the site's typefaces.
  • OpenStreetMap & CARTO — used to display the interactive map of villa locations on the homepage.
  • WhatsApp & Instagram — external platforms we link to for enquiries and updates; any message you send through them is subject to that platform's own privacy policy.

We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information to any other third party.

How we use your information

Information you share with us is used only to respond to your enquiry, discuss availability and pricing, and, where a booking proceeds, to arrange your stay. We retain enquiry correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed for these purposes.

Your rights

Under POPIA, you may ask us to confirm what personal information we hold about you, request a copy of it, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how it's used. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Security

We take reasonable steps to keep any information you share with us secure, and correspondence is handled by a small, trusted team.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time as the site or our services evolve. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact us

For any privacy-related question or request, reach us at xclusivecapetown@gmail.com or 069 986 3597.

This policy is provided as a general guide and does not constitute legal advice. We recommend it be reviewed by a qualified attorney to confirm it fully meets your compliance obligations.
